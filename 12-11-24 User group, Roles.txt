To create a user group just follow the below steps.
go to user gourps
click on Create group
User group name:- give any name 
Add users to the group - Optional (1) :- choose IAMuser 

Attach permissions policies - Optional :- choose any permission policy 
I choosed EC2fullaccess
click on Create user group

Login your IAM user into other browser and check it to insure that, it is work perfectly.

1. Trust policy:- Who can take this role?
2. permission policy :- it is defined as what power will be given after taking role.
