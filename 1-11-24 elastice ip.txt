Go to ec2 service, in left cornor scroll down , in Network & Security section click of Elastice Ips 
to create elastic IP 
click on Allocate Elastic IP address which is present right top cornor.
here is one importance factor was that network border group

and elastice ip created.

Private IP address (select private ip of that instance)
click on Associate
